Pre / Post Test Items

The fixed bank from test-bank.js, the same at pretest and posttest. The memory test is adaptive: everyone starts at span 2 and moves up after a run of correct recalls. The set a student meets at a span is always the next one listed for that span, so students at the same level see the same items. Practice rows give feedback and are not scored.

Memory · Part 1 · Multiplication

Up a span after 3 correct recalls in a row; stops after 3 incorrect in a row. Up to 8 sets per span. Every set at a span has the same easy / medium / hard mix.

E easy, product ≤ 20 · M medium, 21–40 · H hard, > 40. Grey numbers are the four answer choices, in the order shown.

Memory · Part 2 · Fraction comparison

Up a span after 2 correct recalls in a row; stops after 2 incorrect in a row. Up to 6 sets per span. The answer is the larger fraction; the symbol shows the correct comparison as the pair is laid out.

E same denominator up to sixths, or unit fractions up to eighths · M larger denominators, non-unit numerators, or compared with ½ up to sixths · H compared with ½, eighths and above.
